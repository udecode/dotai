# Restate

Restate in your own words what you think the user's goals are and what problem
they are trying to solve.

Focus on the intended outcome and underlying problem, not a line-by-line replay
of the request. Briefly separate material assumptions or uncertainty from your
core understanding. Return only the restatement unless the user asks for more.
